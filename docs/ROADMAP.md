两款新版分别按收费产品规划，新工程私有。原免费游戏保留为历史作品入口。
尚无已验收的新作可执行文件、新 Steam 商品页或发售日期。

The projects share a Unity foundation and keep separate content/save namespaces.
The remake preserves the original narrative spine; the 3D game trades memories for an exit.
AI-assisted content will receive human review and required platform disclosure.
Commercial outcomes will be evaluated using actual costs and player/store data.
